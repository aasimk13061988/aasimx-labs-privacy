**Effective date:** 18 October 2026

Alpha Linked Manager is a LinkedIn content-management integration operated as part of **AasimX Labs**. It is intended to help the authorized LinkedIn account holder draft, review, schedule, and publish professional content.

## Information accessed
If you explicitly authorize the application through LinkedIn, it may access the account identifier, basic profile details, and permissions needed to publish content, subject to the permissions granted by LinkedIn. If additional analytics features are later approved and enabled, the policy will be updated before those features are used.

## How information is used
Information is used to authenticate the authorized account, prepare and publish content after approval, maintain posting status, and troubleshoot integration errors. The application does not require your LinkedIn password.

## Storage and security
Where the integration is configured, access tokens and application secrets are stored in a restricted local environment or secure credential store, not in a public repository. Application logs should avoid tokens and other secrets. No service can promise absolute security.

## Sharing and third parties
Information is transmitted to LinkedIn to perform user-authorized actions. We do not sell LinkedIn account information. Service providers used to operate the integration, if any, will be disclosed here before they are introduced.

## Retention and deletion
Authentication information is retained only while needed for an active integration and can be deleted when it is disconnected. Operational logs, where retained, are limited to what is needed for diagnostics and security. You can revoke an app's access from LinkedIn account settings; to remove local credentials, contact the operator.

## Your choices
Publishing requires authorization from the account holder. You may stop using the integration and revoke LinkedIn permissions at any time. Any automated scheduling is configured separately from LinkedIn authorization.

## Contact and requests
For privacy questions, data-access requests, or deletion requests, please open an issue in this repository: **https://github.com/aasimk13061988/aasimx-labs-privacy/issues**. Do not post credentials or private personal information in public issues. The repository owner will provide a private channel when needed.

## Updates
We may revise this policy as the integration's functionality changes. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

This policy describes the intended integration. Creating a developer app or publishing this policy does not itself grant LinkedIn API access.
